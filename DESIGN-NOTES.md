# Dr. Vargas 48-hour preview design notes

## Concept position

This is a bounded facelift of the existing practice website, not a rebrand or replacement content strategy. It keeps the published wordmark, office photo, phone-first appointment flow, services, patient form, doctor biographies, and linked patient resources while improving hierarchy, mobile scanning, and calls to action. It remains a concept preview and does not change the live practice site.

## Existing-site assets and content used

- Official wordmark: `assets/official-wordmark.png`
- Published optical showroom photo: `assets/practice-optical-showroom.jpg`
- Published VSP and All About Vision resource marks
- Existing service categories: contact lenses, sports eyewear, safety eyewear, and laser surgery consultation
- Existing biographies for Joe A. Vargas, O.D. and Antonio F. Moran, O.D.
- Existing patient history form and published address, phone, and fax

The practice's WordPress media inventory contained one genuine practice photo. The concept uses that image once at its natural aspect ratio and does not invent staff, patient, or office photography.

## Visual and release boundaries

- Georgia display type and system sans body type retain the original concept's readable, low-dependency design.
- The established black-and-gold wordmark is preserved inside the refreshed teal, paper, and gold system.
- No abstract eye or target motif remains in the header or hero.
- Insurance participation, office hours, accessibility, and online scheduling are not asserted without practice confirmation.
- Required checks: 1440 px and 390 px, no overflow, one H1, named controls, valid local assets, visible concept disclosure, and HTTPS after publication.
