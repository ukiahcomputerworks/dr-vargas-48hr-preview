# Dr. Vargas institutional visual-system notes

## Concept position

The page preserves the established practice identity and direct, phone-first appointment flow while presenting the approved patient-facing language in a traditional local medical-practice structure. Public copy contains no redesign-process commentary.

## Existing-site assets and content used

- Owner-supplied office logo: `assets/office-logo.png`, preserved without regeneration or restyling
- Exterior of the practice at 789 South Dora Street: `assets/practice-building-exterior.jpg`, sourced from the Google Street View location image and attributed in the page caption
- Published optical showroom photo retained in the repository as source evidence but no longer used in the hero
- Published VSP and All About Vision resource marks
- Existing service categories: contact lenses, sports eyewear, safety eyewear, and laser surgery consultation
- Existing biographies for Joe A. Vargas, O.D. and Antonio F. Moran, O.D.
- Existing patient history form and published address, phone, and fax
- Published office hours: Monday 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM; Tuesday through Friday 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM; weekends closed

The practice's WordPress media inventory contains one interior showroom photo. The redesigned hero instead uses the identifiable exterior of the practice address so the homepage establishes local recognition immediately. No staff or patient photography is invented.

## Visual and release boundaries

- Georgia headings and Arial body text create a familiar newspaper and medical-record tone.
- The owner-supplied black-and-gold office logo appears as a squared masthead mark, without a rounded black container or transparent-canvas scaling artifact.
- The page uses crisp white, charcoal, deep navy, restrained gold, flat borders, and compact section spacing. It contains no beige gradient, floating hero, glass effect, pill navigation, or heavily rounded imagery.
- The desktop masthead separates identity, address, phone, and navigation; the hero is a fixed split between patient information and the physical building.
- Insurance participation, accessibility, and online scheduling are not asserted without practice confirmation. Published office hours are included with a patient-facing reminder that holiday hours may vary.
- Service cards use benefit-led headings without industrial numbering.
- Required checks: 1440 px and 390 px, no overflow, one H1, named controls, valid local assets, logo inspection at actual header sizes, and HTTPS after publication.
