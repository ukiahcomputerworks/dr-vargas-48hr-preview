Services

Vision care for work, play, and everyday life.

Every patient has different needs. We take the time to understand how you live, work, and use your eyes before recommending the right next step.

Contact lenses fitted for comfort and clarity

Your contact lens evaluation considers your prescription, eye health, and daily visual needs. Dr. Vargas and Dr. Moran fit hard and soft lenses, including bifocal contact lenses.

Protect your eyes without losing sight of the game

Everyday glasses are not designed for sports impact. We can help children and adults choose protective eyewear suited to their sport, prescription, and performance needs.

Reliable protection for work, home, and the outdoors

We offer prescription and non-prescription safety eyewear for the workplace, home projects, hunting, motorcycle riding, and other activities where your eyes need added protection.

Straight answers about laser vision correction

If you are considering LASIK or another refractive procedure, our doctors can evaluate your vision, answer your questions, and explain whether you may be a good candidate.

Not sure which service you need? Call us at 707-462-8363 and our team will be glad to help.

Joe A. Vargas, O.D.

Dr. Vargas has served Mendocino County since 2003. He earned his B.A. from UC Davis and Doctor of Optometry degree from the University of Houston, then returned to Ukiah to open his private practice. He especially values caring for area youth and Spanish-speaking patients.

Antonio F. Moran, O.D.

A UC Berkeley School of Optometry graduate, Dr. Moran is bilingual in English and Spanish and has 18 years of comprehensive vision-care experience. His approach emphasizes questions, clear explanations, and collaboration.
